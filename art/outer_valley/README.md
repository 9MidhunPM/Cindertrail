# Outer Valley art

`outer_land.glb` contains original Cindertrail geometry for Lanternwood, Sunken Aqueduct, Sunwatch Observatory and the roads between them. The limestone arcades, copper survey instrument, staggered tree crowns, trail paving, sluice pools and layered cliffs are authored variations on the supplied valley, side path, crossing and relay ascent illustrations.

Regenerate the mesh with `python3 tools/build_outer_valley.py` from a checkout with NumPy installed. The baker reads the trail graph and landmark coordinates from `data/outer_valley` and uses a fixed seed for small scenery. Godot imports the named terrain, bridge footings, architecture and tree trunks as static collision. Instantiating `scenes/world/outer_valley.tscn` applies the vertex pigments and supplied Grove shallows shader, installs both drawbridges, places the waymarks and creates the three defense sites. The project’s existing limestone, bronze and timber materials supply the moving bridge parts.

To regenerate a journey variant, run `python3 tools/build_outer_valley.py --layout 1`, using layout number 1, 2, or 3. Each bake reads its corresponding `data/outer_valley/layout_N.json` and writes `outer_land_N.glb`. The camp architecture, materials and terrain-building code are shared across the four layouts. Each exported mesh follows its own roads, cuts its own bridge channels and leaves openings for its quarry, grove and relay districts.

The node IDs `west`, `south` and `east` identify Lanternwood, Sunken Aqueduct and Sunwatch Observatory respectively in every graph. They date from the original valley. The physical west trail out of the quarry leads to Lanternwood in layout 1, Sunwatch Observatory in layout 2 and Sunken Aqueduct in layout 3. The trail endpoints, signs, local architecture, biome, and interactable landmarks all resolve from the selected graph.

Version-two journeys add three independently selected trail connections at Pilgrims’ Cross, Orchard Pass and Mineral Ward. Each fork joins different road arms depending on the seed. The raised limestone, timber and copper spans have their own imported walk surfaces. Only the selected spans appear on the map and receive waymarks.

Run `python3 tools/build_trail_forks.py` after rebuilding a base valley to update its six fork choices. The baker surveys the existing ground and static obstacles, writes the meshes in `art/outer_valley/forks`, and records the corresponding polylines in `data/outer_valley/trail_forks.json`. The paving, piers and bronze parapets are original procedural geometry informed by the supplied side-path and valley stills.
