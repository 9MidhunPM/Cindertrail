# Traveler’s Reckoning prologue paintings

Eight original plates were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool. The four earlier plates follow them in the playable prologue. Captions and navigation are live Godot UI. The original comic and corrected stills guided the painterly treatment, silhouettes, and palette. The game uses WebP copies of the generated plates.

## Shared prompt

> Use case illustration-story. New full-bleed 16:9 widescreen single painted narrative panel for original indie game CINDERTRAIL. Match an illustrated fieldbook of a ruined alpine valley, painterly gouache grain, crisp silhouette, focused compositions, aged creamy limestone, oxidized copper, rusty orange leaves, deep cobalt, shaded teal trees and warm saddle gold. Cinematic clarity, old-world engineering, no modern objects, no title, no writing or watermark, no letterboxes or panels within the image. Each painting stands as an editorial plate with action clearly readable at 720p.

## Page prompts

1. **The age of roads.** Historic wide landscape establishing scene. An inhabited headwater valley in its age of roads, with ivory arcades and narrow inhabited terraced market towns, orange trees, a pale bridge across a turquoise river, small couriers crossing, pale morning sun. A ring-shaped beacon on the distant top ridge catches natural sunrise. It is a living society before the collapse; warm atmosphere, memorable regional landmarks.
2. **The seven shrines.** Inside a roofless ancient circular workshop above the river, masked limestone engineers are cutting seven small solar channels into a huge circular raised bronze-and-stone mechanism, the channels converge toward an empty mount and the stone points are arranged radially. The light is a few warm lines, there is no miniature sun object. Foreground hand pointing over the mechanism, cream masonry, real fabric and metal. The bright center makes direction and mechanism immediately legible.
3. **The failed dawn.** Wide silhouette shot beneath the upper relay tower in Cindertrail. Lightning-like slate-blue fracture breaks the great bronze flywheel and snaps cables at the exact instant dawn stalls. A thin amber stripe on the horizon dies against deep advancing cobalt night, distant town lanterns shut out. Atmospheric narrative of a failed mechanism, avoid explosions and giant glowing suns, use few clear shapes.
4. **Broken Quarry.** Broken Quarry last rescue effort action shot. Two work crews in period teal and rust cloth silhouettes try to haul a heavy limestone counterweight using one tall timber crane and copper rope. In the foreground a cracked ramp breaks in the blue moonlight; the load is safely visible above an empty gorge and one torn flag curls in the wind. Tall escarpments, hammer-marked stone, readable rope tension, no text.
5. **The flood.** Storm after midnight over Flooded Grove and the Sunken Aqueduct. A collapsing pale channel sends a short cascade into a teal grove of massive canopies while the bronze bell arcade disappears below rising turquoise water, a ripple centered on its keystone, a frightened pale heron rises. Horizontal action, clear water plane, copper debris in foreground, multiple arches in depth. This is the historical sinking, water rises around the ruin.
6. **The empty towns.** Abandoned limestone ward after its inhabitants left. Framed low through dark wooden eaves and hanging vines, a dark crooked stall and cracked market arch, tarnished shutter, wet cobbled street descending to empty terraced houses and violet blue forest vapour, a few amber leaves and tiny animal track glints. The loneliness of a former city, signs as blank weathered wood, no person visible.
7. **Vesper’s oath.** The Vesper Colossus stands sentinel on the high beacon steps, an animated ancient limestone guardian with broad segmented fists, asymmetrical broken copper crown, violet stone fault lines and closed split chest armor. Low-angle shot sees its weathered enormous silhouette dwarfed by the empty ring-shaped beacon behind. Deep silver-blue moonlight, small gold keeper's lantern at its feet, high alpine ridges, no core and no traveler. Somber oath and duty.
8. **The last keepers.** Last Lantern and Sunwatch Observatory under a crescent. Two last keepers in simple traveler-like teal cloaks high on a ruined terrace aligning copper mirrors. One tiny amber wick sparks in dark blue, catches a sight line toward distant weathered observatory ring above snowy mountains. Close foreground hands and familiar lantern silhouette, foreground broken cream arch, aged route papers with no legible writing, contrast a fragile enduring light with the long night.
