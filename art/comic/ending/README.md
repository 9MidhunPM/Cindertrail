# The Last Sunrise paintings

Three original background plates generated for Cindertrail with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. They are 1672 × 941 WebP textures. Godot animates the camera drift, light, and transitions over them. The playable traveler and sun core appear in the live shots before and after these paintings.

The opening comic and the corrected sunrise concept supplied the palette and architectural direction. The three generations used these prompts.

## Shared prompt

> Use case illustration-story. A production background plate for the original CINDERTRAIL motion comic finale. Landscape widescreen 16:9 artwork, cinematic game key art, 2D hand-painted with crisp silhouettes and textured gouache brushwork, deep Prussian blue atmosphere and ink-blue shadows, restrained turquoise and rusty copper, weathered warm cream limestone, antique gold first sunlight. Clear foreground/middle/background depth as a 3-layer parallax composition. Rich grounded composition, elegant, refined, cinematic, tactile. This is an ancient abandoned valley, late night at its first sunrise. Paint the light traveling from distant right to left across the scene. Single composition, full bleed, zero text, no borders or overlay, no people, no hero, no magic sun object.

## Opening arch

> GROUND-LEVEL WIDE SHOT THROUGH AN ARCH. Huge intact but weatherworn Romanesque limestone arch frames the edges and top of a vista into a flooded alpine valley. Burnt orange maple leaves and dark ivy at the left foreground, a cracked stone path and toppled carved capital at lower edge. Beyond the arch, starlit cobalt mountains and distant stepped ruins. Still deep midnight blue overall. A single soft band of the very first amber light grazes the cream keystone and spills down the right arch face while its far face remains cold. No huge visible sun, focus on the first glint and quiet depth.

## Flooded Grove

> LOW WIDESCREEN SHOT ACROSS THE FLOODED GROVE. Shallow sparkling jade water, looping creamy limestone causeway of broad worn slabs, distinct cream arches and pillars of different depths, weathered teal canopy and rust-orange leaf litter, white waterfall in soft midground, dark silhouetted reeds and tiny pads low on the left foreground. A few elegant ivory herons lift into the deep indigo sky at upper right, wings open, reflected gold streak lays itself across the water in front alongside cool water. Readable, natural wetland, ancient space waking, balanced half twilight half gold.

## Abandoned ward

> WIDE HIGH VANTAGE OVER AN ABANDONED LIMESTONE TOWN with narrow sloping streets, weathered towers and burnt-copper roofs, cracked roofless gable houses, a tall ruined bridge, trailing green ivy. Cascade of clustered steep houses recedes to a wooded valley and layered blue-gray alpine skyline. Use silhouette of a broken foundry roof in foreground left, first real warm rays breaking across several distant roof ridges from right to left, through blue mist and slate ruins, no implied human inhabitants. More gold than midnight, transitioning out of blue into warm morning. The rooftops remain recognizably ruined.
