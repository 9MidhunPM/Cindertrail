# Asset provenance and credits

## Project visual references

The 18 concept images were generated during the Cindertrail design conversation. Sixteen corrected/canonical images are in `art/concept/`; the two earlier variants are retained in `art/archive/`. They are visual references rather than extracted UV textures or implemented gameplay screenshots. The trailer was supplied by the user and is included unchanged.

## Authored starter resources

The four opening comic panels were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool for Cindertrail. The project keeps the cropped panels and art brief in `art/comic/`.

The palette, Godot material/shader resources, procedural Blender builder, exported static starter meshes and documentation were prepared for this project. The models are editable starting points; no existing game's characters or source models are included.

The living valley uses authored brush grain in the Blender-generated models and a tileable procedural surface mask. The terrain, map assets, and shared textures are reproducible through the builders in `scripts/` and `tools/`.

## Downloaded optional surface maps

ambientCG: `PavingStones036`, `Metal032`, `Fabric030`, `WoodFloor050`. These downloads use CC0 1.0 Universal. Exact downloaded files, source URLs, dates and SHA-256 checksums are in `art/textures/third_party/ambientCG/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`, with companion `CREDITS.md`.

Official license: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Kenney, Quaternius and KayKit packs are recommended sources in the research guide; their entire asset packs are not silently included here. The project does not include installed third-party MCP servers or public skill repositories. Their primary-source links and setup instructions are documented separately.
