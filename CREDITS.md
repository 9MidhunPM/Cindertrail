# Asset provenance and credits

## Project visual references

The 18 concept images were generated during the Cindertrail design conversation. Sixteen corrected/canonical images are in `art/concept/`; the two earlier variants are retained in `art/archive/`. They are visual references rather than extracted UV textures or implemented gameplay screenshots. The trailer was supplied by the user and is included unchanged.

## Authored starter resources

The four opening comic panels were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool for Cindertrail. The project keeps the cropped panels and art brief in `art/comic/`.

The three finale paintings were generated for Cindertrail with the same built-in image tool. The beacon score was composed for the 26-second timeline and is reproducible from its Python source. [Finale art and prompts](art/comic/ending/README.md) · [Score source](tools/score_last_sunrise.py)

The palette, Godot material/shader resources, procedural Blender builder, exported static starter meshes and documentation were prepared for this project. The models are editable starting points; no existing game's characters or source models are included.

The living valley's surface masks and six painted workshop trims are original procedural artwork. The material source lists the maps, channels, and generation command. [Valley paint](art/textures/stylized/README.md)

The layered traveler and its 2K atlas were authored for Cindertrail in `scripts/hero/build_traveler.py` and `scripts/hero/traveler_atlas.py`. The articulated monster roster and painted mineral atlases are generated by `scripts/hero/build_bestiary.py`. Eight wildlife models use vertex-painted coats, feathers, scales, and markings from `tools/build_wildlife.py`. The creature calls, guardian cues, and extraction hum are synthesized from the original score in `tools/build_bestiary_audio.py`.

The alternate valley terrain and collision meshes are original exports from `tools/build_outer_valley.py` and the three authored trail networks in `data/outer_valley/`. The Sunsteel sword choreography, blade trails, and charge and release sounds were authored on the Cindertrail traveler rig.

Mote's original dialogue was voiced with Kokoro-82M. [Voice and generation credits](art/audio/mote/README.md)

## Downloaded music and sound

The six valley cues are edited loops of Scott Buckley's CC BY 4.0 recordings. The foley blends CC0 sounds by Kenney and Rubberduck. See the [track credits](art/audio/score/README.md) and [recording sources](art/audio/foley/README.md).

## Downloaded optional surface maps

ambientCG: `PavingStones036`, `Metal032`, `Fabric030`, `WoodFloor050`. These downloads use CC0 1.0 Universal. Exact downloaded files, source URLs, dates and SHA-256 checksums are in `art/textures/third_party/ambientCG/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`, with companion `CREDITS.md`.

Official license: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Kenney, Quaternius and KayKit packs are recommended sources in the research guide; their entire asset packs are not silently included here. The project does not include installed third-party MCP servers or public skill repositories. Their primary-source links and setup instructions are documented separately.
