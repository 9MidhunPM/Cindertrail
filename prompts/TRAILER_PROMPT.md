# Cindertrail Trailer Prompt

Use the traveler/shrine sheet for character and object continuity and the corresponding site art for each shot. For controllable edits, generate six short clips with shared references and assemble them. Prefer captured gameplay once the playable slice is ready.

```text
Create a 30-second 16:9 concept trailer for CINDERTRAIL: CARRY THE LAST SUNRISE, a stylized 3D open-world adventure with portable-base survival and tower defense. Use a cinematic 24 fps presentation and the supplied art as the visual reference.

The story: one teal-cloaked traveler carries a damaged miniature sun through a dying valley. They choose sites, unfold the shrine, arrange defenses, absorb energy, retreat before being overwhelmed and restore daylight.

Look: matte faceted low-poly geometry, cream stone ruins, weathered bronze machinery, muted orange foliage, deep cobalt shadows, teal forests, turquoise shallow water and warm golden shrine light. Restrained bloom, clear silhouettes and readable action.

Continuity: one faceless traveler in a short weathered teal hooded cloak, charcoal trousers, boots and a bronze-trimmed harness. Exactly ONE sun shrine: bronze tripod cage with a golden sphere and three broken orbital rings. It folds into a lantern on the traveler's back. When deployed or delivered, the harness is EMPTY. Towers are a three-pronged brass bolt turret, cyan mushroom slowing emitter and stone cup mortar. Enemies are black crawlers with cyan fissures and larger stone-armored brutes with violet fissures.

0–5 seconds: shoulder-height third-person camera follows the traveler through a ruined arch onto branching valley trails. Quarry, flooded grove and relay are reachable landmarks.

5–10 seconds: the traveler deploys at the quarry. The camera smoothly rises into a fixed elevated angled defense view of the SAME terrain. A turret fires through a narrow entrance and mortar rounds strike clustered crawlers.

10–15 seconds: the attack intensifies. Traveler recalls defenses, folds and carries the shrine. Camera returns behind them as they run along an open forest escape route with a few pursuers.

15–20 seconds: third-person approach to the grove; the shrine unfolds on a dry island. Camera rises into tactical view. Pursuers enter shallow water and visibly slow while cyan slowing field and amber fire protect the shrine.

20–25 seconds: exposed relay site, enemies along three approaches. Generator energy flows into the sun; its three broken rings reconnect into a restored structure.

25–30 seconds: traveler places the single restored sun in the hilltop beacon; backpack harness stays empty. Pull back over the same valley as dawn light reaches quarry, grove and relay. End with negative space for an editor-added title.

Keep spatial relationships clear. No random costume changes, duplicate cores, active turrets while packed, unreadable generated HUD text or frantic camera shake. Add title typography in editing.

Audio if supported: quiet wind, boots on stone, metallic shrine hum, crisp turret fire, rising percussion during retreat and warm musical release at sunrise. No dialogue.
```

Suggested editor captions: **Choose your ground. / Hold the light. / Know when to run. / Carry the sunrise.**
